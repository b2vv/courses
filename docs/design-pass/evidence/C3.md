## Environment finding (not a product defect)

Building or restarting the frontend with only `docker-compose.yml` + the Kiri override (without `docker-compose.e2e.yml`) gives an enforcing CSP (axe cannot be injected) and drops the `PUBLIC_WAYFORPAY_PAY_HOST` build argument (the stub checkout fails). The Kiri `environment.sh` already sets the right `COMPOSE_FILE`; overriding it by hand is what broke it.
